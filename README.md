# Fluent Books — free books catalog

The catalog of free books for the Fluent Books reader (iPhone, iPad, Mac): classics in English and
picture books for first readers, each with its level and word counts, so the app can show "% of words
you know" before the download.

- `manifest.json` (GitHub Pages) names the current `catalog-*.json` and `counts-*.json` with their
  sizes and SHA-256; the books and covers are files of the [`books` release](../../releases/tag/books).
- The classics are in the public domain: everyone who wrote, translated or illustrated them died
  more than 70 years ago.
- Editions from [Standard Ebooks](https://standardebooks.org) are dedicated to the public domain
  (CC0 1.0) and are published unchanged, with their colophon.
- The children's books marked "public-domain transcription" are texts transcribed by volunteers
  of Project Gutenberg, published without its licence and name, as that licence provides.
- The picture books ("Easy books") are from [StoryWeaver](https://storyweaver.org.in) (Pratham Books),
  [Book Dash](https://bookdash.org) and the [African Storybook Initiative](https://www.africanstorybook.org),
  as published by the [Global Digital Library](https://digitallibrary.io), under Creative Commons
  Attribution licences (CC BY, or CC BY-SA where a book says so; never NonCommercial or
  NoDerivatives). Each book's entry in `catalog-*.json` names its creators, its licence and its
  source, and a page at the end of each EPUB does the same. Their only change: each is converted to a
  reflowable e-book with one table-of-contents entry, and the publisher's own credit pages are
  replaced by that page; the text and the pictures are unchanged.
- The catalog files themselves (levels, counts, and the descriptions we wrote) are dedicated to the
  public domain under CC0 1.0 (see LICENSE); the credit lines of the picture books are theirs and
  stay under their licences.
