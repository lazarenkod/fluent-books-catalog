# Fluent Books — free books catalog

The catalog of free books for the Fluent Books reader (iPhone, iPad, Mac): classics in English
from picture books to novels, each with its level and word counts, so the app can show "% of words
you know" before the download.

- `manifest.json` (GitHub Pages) names the current `catalog-*.json` and `counts-*.json` with their
  sizes and SHA-256; the books and covers are files of the [`books` release](../../releases/tag/books).
- Every book is in the public domain: everyone who wrote, translated or illustrated it died more
  than 70 years ago.
- Editions from [Standard Ebooks](https://standardebooks.org) are dedicated to the public domain
  (CC0 1.0) and are published unchanged, with their colophon.
- The children's books marked "public-domain transcription" are texts transcribed by volunteers
  of Project Gutenberg, published without its licence and name, as that licence provides.
- The catalog files themselves (descriptions, levels, counts) are dedicated to the public domain
  under CC0 1.0 (see LICENSE).
